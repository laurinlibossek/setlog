# Store listing

Texts for App Store Connect and the Google Play Console, in English and German, plus the answers to the stores' privacy, health and age questions. Each heading has the field's limit in brackets and the draft's length after the dot, counted in characters the way both stores count them ("ü" and "•" are one each).

The texts follow the app's own style: short, plain, sentence case, and "du" in German. A few things they deliberately leave out:

- **Strong isn't named anywhere** (Laurin, 3 Nov 2026). The import line says "the CSV export of other workout apps".
- **No promises beyond what the app does today:** no Apple Health, no Watch, no sync.

## The stores' questions

### App Store: App Privacy

**Data Not Collected.** Apple counts data as "collected" when it leaves the device so that you or someone else can access it. Increment sends nothing anywhere: backups and CSV files only go where the user sends them through the share sheet, and that's the user's doing, not collection. Data Apple gathers itself (downloads, crash reports from people who opted in) doesn't go on the label.

### App Store: age rating

Answer "None" or "No" to the questions about violence, mature content, gambling, user-generated content, messaging and web access. A template link is shared through the system share sheet to an app the user picks, not inside Increment, so it isn't messaging or user-generated content.

**Health or Wellness Topics: Yes** (Laurin, 3 Nov 2026), because Apple's examples include exercise recommendations and Increment ships the example templates Push, Pull and Legs and suggests the next workout. That should give **9+** instead of 4+; App Store Connect shows the result as you answer. "Medical or Treatment Information" stays No.

### App Store: export compliance

The app has no encryption of its own, so the answer is "None of the algorithms mentioned". `app.json` has `"config": { "usesNonExemptEncryption": false }` under `ios`, which answers it for every upload.

### Google Play: Data safety

- Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? **No.** Google doesn't count data that's processed only on the device and never sent off it.
- That ends the form. The store page then shows "No data collected" and "No data shared with third parties".

### Google Play: other App content forms

- **Health apps declaration:** every app has to fill it in, even one with no health features. Tick the activity and fitness category (workout tracking). Body weight is logged as part of training, not for diet or weight-loss programmes, so the nutrition and weight management category probably doesn't apply. Leave the medical ones unticked.
- **Ads:** no ads.
- **App access:** everything is available without logging in.
- **Content rating (IARC questionnaire):** category "All Other App Types", then "No" to everything (violence, sexuality, language, controlled substances, gambling, users interacting, sharing location). Expected: PEGI 3, USK 0, Everyone.
- **Target audience:** 13 and over (13–15, 16–17, 18+). Leaving out under-13s keeps the app outside Google's Families policy, which would add requirements a gym log doesn't need.
- **Government app, financial features, news app:** no.

### Google Play: before the first release

A personal developer account (created after Nov 2023) can only publish to production after a **closed test with at least 12 testers who stay opted in for 14 days in a row**. Internal testing doesn't count. Organisation accounts are exempt. Friends with Android phones and a Google account are enough, but plan the two weeks in.
